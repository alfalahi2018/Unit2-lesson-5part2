Teacher plan · 25 minutes

Class: Grade 7 · Unit 2: Awesome animals · Lesson 5, part 2 (Activity Book: Vocabulary and Grammar)
Lesson aims: to use animal verbs in context and to use the first conditional.
Target language: bite, sting, roar, fight, dive, dig, hide, lay eggs, curl up, fly away, run away, die; first conditional.
Materials: Unit 2 flashcards (animal verbs), Activity Book pages 108–109, device or projector, Photocopiables 2, 7 and 11 (extra).

TimeStageWhat happensTeacher’s Book link
0–4Warm-up
Spot 1: Guess the Action		Students guess the animal verb from a picture, as in the Guessing game with the verb flashcards.“Starting the lesson”: play the Guessing game (pictures) using the Unit 2 flashcards (animal verbs) as prompts.
4–10Formative 1
Spot 2: Read and Circle		Students read facts about the pill bug and circle the correct verb.Activity 1: read and circle the correct options.
10–16Formative 2
Spot 3: Listen and Complete		Ask if students recognise the animal; explain that pill bugs are crustaceans, related to shrimps and crabs. Students listen to a presentation and complete the sentences.Activity 2 (2.12): listen to a presentation about pill bugs, then complete the sentences.
16–22Formative 3
Spot 4: If … Will!		Students complete first conditional sentences with the correct verb forms. Remind: if + present simple, will + infinitive.Activity 3: read and complete the sentences using the first conditional.
22–25Closing
Spot 5: Facts and a Fib		Students find the fib about pill bugs, then write their own first conditional sentence. Exit ticket.Activities 4–5 (own ideas, compare with a partner) and “Finishing the lesson”: Three facts and a fib technique.

Differentiation

  • Support 🌱: choices instead of typing in If … Will!, the audio script is shown, only two options in the guessing game, and sentence frames in the closing.
  • Standard 🌿: full activities, script behind a button (fewer points if used).
  • Challenge 🌳: typing without hints, two plays of the recording, and three own sentences.

Notes and keys

  • Listen and Complete key: water, die, eggs, curl up, leaves, sting.
  • If … Will! key: feels / will curl up · gets / will die · look / will see · touch / won’t bite · sees / will fly away.
  • The recording is read by the browser (text to speech) from a short script written for this game. Play your own Class Book/Activity Book audio 2.12 too, and compare the reading sentences with Activity Book pages 108–109.
  • Deaf/hard of hearing students: use the Script button and written answers.
  • Extra activities: Photocopiables 2, 7 and 11.
